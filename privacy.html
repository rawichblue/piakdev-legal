App: af-studio · Facebook Page “ไอ้เปี๊ยกที่เป็น Dev” · Instagram @piakdev · Effective 27 September 2026

English

af-studio is a private tool used by one person, the owner of the Facebook Page “ไอ้เปี๊ยกที่เป็น Dev” and the Instagram account @piakdev. It uploads the owner's own short videos, with their captions, to that Page and that Instagram account through the Meta Graph API.

  • What we collect from other people: nothing. The app does not log in visitors, read their profiles, messages or comments, or collect any personal data from anyone other than the owner.
  • What the app uses: the owner's Page ID, Instagram account ID and a Page access token, needed to publish the owner's videos. They are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to Meta to make the post.
  • Cookies and tracking: none.
  • Third parties: the videos are published on Facebook and Instagram and are subject to Meta's own policies.
  • Deleting data: see Data deletion instructions.
  • Contact: rawich.itp@gmail.com
